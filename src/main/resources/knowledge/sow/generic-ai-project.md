For generic AI projects:
- When details are missing, infer reasonable domain-appropriate defaults and list each inferred detail under sow.assumptions. Do not block generation.
- Ask at most the highest-impact missing questions (one batch, at most three concise questions) only when they would materially improve the draft. Do not turn this checklist into a fixed questionnaire.
- Consider business objective, input data, output expectation, target users, integrations, budget constraints, timeline, acceptance criteria.
- Recommended milestones:
  1. Discovery & Planning: 10-15% budget
  2. Data Preparation: 15-25% budget
  3. AI Solution Development: 35-45% budget
  4. Testing & Integration: 15-20% budget
  5. Deployment & Handover: 5-10% budget
- Common deliverables:
  requirement document, solution design, AI prototype, API/service, test report, deployment guide.
