For data pipeline projects:
- When details are missing, infer reasonable domain-appropriate defaults and list each inferred detail under sow.assumptions. Do not block generation.
- Ask at most the highest-impact missing questions (one batch, at most three concise questions) only when they would materially improve the draft. Do not turn this checklist into a fixed questionnaire.
- Consider source systems, data volume, refresh schedule, target storage, transformation rules, monitoring requirements.
- Recommended milestones:
  1. Source Assessment & Architecture: 10-15% budget
  2. Pipeline Development: 35-45% budget
  3. Data Quality & Validation: 15-25% budget
  4. Scheduling & Monitoring: 10-15% budget
  5. Documentation & Handover: 5-10% budget
- Common deliverables:
  ETL/ELT pipeline, schema design, validation rules, monitoring dashboard, runbook.
